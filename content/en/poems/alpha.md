---
title: "Alpha"
date: 2025-12-29
---

<div class="poem">
to shadows melting into light.
The sea keeps ancient voices still,
names that were never spoken aloud.
On memory’s narrow path

I walk without a map or time,
and the heart — a small lantern —
flares for all I feared to live.

If tomorrow the dream is lost,
let the truth remain unclothed:
that even when we fall silent,
love always finds a way to speak.
</div>
